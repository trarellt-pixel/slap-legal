App Support

S.L.A.P.  ·  Last updated September 4, 2026

This is the support page for the S.L.A.P. iOS app. If something in the app is broken, or you have a question about your closet data, start here.

Contact us

S.L.A.P.’s support inbox is hello@slap.style. Email that address and include:

  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead

We read support email and reply as quickly as we can, usually within two business days.

Your closet data

Your closet is stored on your device. When the app is connected, it may also sync to S.L.A.P. servers so it can be restored.

To delete the server copy, open the app and go to Settings → Privacy → Delete my S.L.A.P. data. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy. If you only delete the app, the server copy remains until you use that in-app control or email hello@slap.style.
