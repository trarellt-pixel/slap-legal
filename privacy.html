Privacy Policy

Last updated September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the S.L.A.P. iOS app (“we,” “us”). It is the policy linked from App Store Connect and from inside the app.

Who we are

S.L.A.P. (Signature Looks. Elevated Presence.) is a personal styling app from Fortress Technologies. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@slap.style.

What we collect

S.L.A.P. creates a device account automatically. You do not need an email address or password. We use a random device identifier so your closet can sync when a S.L.A.P. server is available.

Your first name and style questionnaire answers, if you provide them, stay on this device unless you type them into a look note.

Photos and your closet

If you photograph an outfit or scan a closet piece, we use that photo to score the look, suggest how to raise the score, and remember pieces in your closet. When a S.L.A.P. server is available, those photos and your closet upload so we can restore your closet and support stylist features.

Retailer items you browse are shopping links only. They are never automatically saved to your closet.

Your activity in the app

Scored looks, closet pieces, and style preferences are stored on this device and, when the app is connected, on S.L.A.P. servers tied to your device account.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with retailers.

AI providers

To score looks and suggest improvements, S.L.A.P. may send photos to AI providers — xAI, OpenAI, or Anthropic. Those providers process the photos solely to produce a score and suggestions. How long they retain that data is governed by their own rules.

Shopping links

If you tap a house such as Fashion Nova, Cole Haan, or Nike, you leave S.L.A.P. and visit that retailer’s website. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

No tracking or analytics

S.L.A.P. does not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or third-party data collection tools. We do not use your data for tracking across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy → Delete my S.L.A.P. data. That wipes the server copy tied to your device account.

Deleting the S.L.A.P. app from your device removes the on-device copy. If you only delete the app, the server copy remains until you use the in-app delete control or email hello@slap.style.

Children

S.L.A.P. is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@slap.style.
