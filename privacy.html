Privacy Policy

Last updated August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the S.L.A.P. iOS app (“we,” “us”). It is the policy linked from App Store Connect and from inside the app.

Who we are

S.L.A.P. (Signature Looks. Elevated Presence.) is a personal styling app from Fortress Technologies. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@slap.style.

What we collect

S.L.A.P. does not create an account and does not require an email address to use the app. Your first name and style questionnaire answers, if you provide them, are stored only on this device.

Photos and your closet

If you photograph an outfit or scan a closet piece, that photo stays on this device. We use it to score the look, suggest how to raise the score, and remember pieces in your closet. This version does not upload those photos to a S.L.A.P. server. Retailer items you browse are shopping links only — they are never saved to your closet unless you photograph your own clothes.

Your activity in the app

Scored looks, closet pieces, and style preferences are stored locally using standard iOS app storage. None of this activity is transmitted to a S.L.A.P. account database, because this version does not operate one. We cannot see your closet, and we do not sell or share it, because we never receive it.

Shopping links

If you tap a house such as Fashion Nova, Cole Haan, or Nike, you leave S.L.A.P. and visit that retailer’s website. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

No tracking or analytics

S.L.A.P. does not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or third-party data collection tools. We do not use your data for tracking across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, deleting the S.L.A.P. app from your device permanently deletes your closet, looks, and style profile with it. There is no separate account or server copy to remove, and there is no additional in-app account-deletion request because no account exists.

Children

S.L.A.P. is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@slap.style.
